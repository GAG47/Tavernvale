# v3.0.1 World-scale Subsurface Strata

## Responsibility

This version adds the world-scale background geology below every World Cell. It answers which major rock material occupies an absolute `z`, while leaving groundwater, caves, resources, geothermal features, and magma to later systems.

The generator consumes `SpatialGraph`, `WorldCompositionLayer`, the final Hydrology-conditioned `TerrainHeightLayer`, `GeologyLayer`, `world_seed`, and `SubsurfaceStrataSettings`. It produces `SubsurfaceStrataLayer` and does not mutate its inputs.

## Formal data and queries

`SubsurfaceStrataLayer` stores CSR-like PackedArrays:

- `cell_offsets: PackedInt32Array`, with `cell_count + 1` entries;
- `material_ids: PackedInt32Array`;
- `top_z: PackedFloat32Array`.

Each Cell has one to four records. Record zero exactly matches the Cell's Geology surface material and final Terrain height. Successive `top_z` values strictly descend; the final record extends to negative infinity.

The formal query API is `record_range_for_cell`, `material_at_z`, `layer_index_at_z`, and `layer_bounds`. A query above the surface returns `NO_MATERIAL`; invalid Cell/layer queries return safe sentinels rather than indexing invalid memory.

## Stable seed domain and coherent noise

The master salt is `0x53545241` (`STRA`), mixed with `world_seed` through `DeterministicRng.stable_mix`. Three sequence channels use `SQ00`, `SQ01`, and `SQ02`; three thickness channels use `TH00`, `TH01`, and `TH02`. Each channel is independently mixed from the master seed, so the generator has no shared RNG state and cannot shift another generator's randomness.

The three sequence and three thickness fields use `FastNoiseLite.TYPE_SIMPLEX_SMOOTH`, FBM with two octaves, gain `0.50`, and lacunarity `2.0`. Sequence feature scale is 450 world units; thickness feature scale is 300 world units. Those samples are mapped directly from `[-1, 1]` to `[0, 1]` without per-world normalization. The later Deep Crust channel is documented in the final correction section below.

## Province sequences

- Oceanic Crust ends in Volcanic Rock. Marine Sedimentary Rock and any other nonterminal surface material transition directly to Volcanic Rock.
- Craton sedimentary, volcanic, or marine surfaces enter Metamorphic Rock at selector `< 0.35`, otherwise Crystalline Rock.
- Orogenic Belt uses the same structure with a `< 0.80` Metamorphic threshold.
- Sedimentary Basin permits one surface sediment-to-sediment transition, then enters Metamorphic or Crystalline basement. Surface Volcanic and Marine Sedimentary materials use their locked v3.0.1 routes.
- Passive Margin permits its locked surface sediment alternation and then enters Metamorphic or Crystalline basement.
- Volcanic Province routes sediment toward Volcanic, Metamorphic, or Crystalline rock and routes Volcanic toward Metamorphic or Crystalline rock.

On continental Provinces, Metamorphic always transitions to Crystalline and Crystalline is terminal. Adjacent duplicate materials are rejected.

## Thickness

Province base thicknesses are Oceanic 20, Craton 24, Orogenic 30, Sedimentary Basin 36, Passive Margin 34, and Volcanic Province 30. Finite layers use depth factors `1.00`, `1.10`, and `1.20` and coherent noise factor `lerp(0.75, 1.25, selector)`. Results are clamped to `[12, 55]`. Terminal layers have no finite thickness.

The existing composition debug scene initially had a Subsurface Strata page with seven views. Those seven were rendered and inspected at Seed 1 / Continents / 20,000 Cells.

- Layer Count shows large coherent regions rather than Cell-scale speckle. Craton-dominated areas are mostly simple, while Basin and Passive Margin areas visibly contain richer three- and four-layer columns.
- At `z=-25`, air is clearly separated and shallow sediment/metamorphic regions remain extensive. At `z=-75`, deep Volcanic oceanic crust and Crystalline continental cores dominate while coherent intermediate regions remain. At `z=-150`, oceanic areas are Volcanic and continental areas are Crystalline, matching terminal semantics.
- The 25%, 50%, and 75% transects contain only a few major layers. Matching sequences use linearly connected boundaries; genuine sequence changes remain visible as lateral transitions. No obvious parameter defect was found in that initial pass, so no aesthetic retuning was performed.

## Final core correction: Deep Substrate

The final v3.0.1 correction adds `WorldCompositionLayer` as a formal generator and Validator input. `composition.continental_value[cell_id]`, rather than final Terrain land/water status, is the macro-scale source for deep continental versus oceanic substrate. The formal `SubsurfaceStrataLayer` remains unchanged; the terminal Material continues to carry the resulting fact.

An independent Deep Crust noise channel uses salt `0x44435255` (`DCRU`), mixed from the existing `STRA` base seed. It uses Simplex Smooth FBM, two octaves, gain `0.50`, lacunarity `2.0`, no domain warp, and feature scale `800.0`. Its raw `[-1, 1]` value is not normalized. The fixed calculation is:

`deep_score = continental_value + deep_noise * 4.0`

Scores at least `14.0` are Continental Deep Substrate; lower scores are Oceanic Deep Substrate. Settings expose and validate feature scale `800.0`, amplitude `4.0`, and threshold `14.0`. No parameter was retuned after visual inspection.

Continental Provinces retain their original sequences and Crystalline terminal. Oceanic Crust now has two routes:

- Oceanic Deep Substrate retains Marine Sedimentary → Volcanic terminal, or Volcanic terminal.
- Continental Deep Substrate uses Marine Sedimentary → Volcanic → Crystalline terminal, or Volcanic → Crystalline terminal.

Generator and Validator share the same deterministic transition-allowance helper. The Validator independently recreates the DCRU field from graph positions, composition, world seed, and settings, then checks both each transition and the expected terminal.
