# v2.3.2 — Localized Arcane Forcing

Date: 2026-09-03

## Why v2.3.1 needed correction

v2.3.1 correctly established persistent Natural Sources and Sinks, but each Site directly applied a local rate across a 140-unit radius. That made one Site a roughly 280-unit-wide forcing area: the origin, direct affected extent, and initial radial shape were all chosen by the forcing object itself. The result was a set of large circular Mana bumps and pits.

A Source or Sink should identify where non-equilibrium enters or leaves the material world. Diffusion, Flowability, Arcane Web, Arcane Circulation, Background Mana, and Background Restoration should determine how far that non-equilibrium spreads and what shape it ultimately takes. The physical size of the defect and the radius of its final anomaly are therefore separate concepts.

## Formal model

`ArcaneForcingSite` now has exactly:

- `id`
- `world_position`
- `kind`
- `core_radius`
- `total_power`

The old `radius` and `strength` fields were removed without aliases. Every normal Site uses `core_radius = 30.0` world units and `total_power = 70.0` Mana amount per solver time. Position, polarity, the `FRCE` seed domain, deterministic Bridson sampling, minimum separation 420, `k = 30`, and the 420-unit generation margin are unchanged. Site position remains independent of Web, Circulation, Flowability, Stability, and Background Mana.

`core_radius` is the physical support of the localized exchange defect, not its final environmental influence radius. `total_power` is the complete Site's maximum base injection or extraction capacity, not a per-Cell rate.

## Localized kernel and fixed total power

For distance `d`, core radius `R`, `x = d/R`, and `u = 1-x`:

```text
kernel(d) = 0,                              d >= R
kernel(d) = u²(3 - 2u),                    d < R
```

The full circular support has the analytical integral:

```text
Z = integral(kernel dA) = 3πR²/10 = 0.3πR²
```

The projected base rate density is:

```text
base_rate_density = total_power * kernel / Z
```

Consequently, changing `core_radius` changes the area and density through which Mana enters or leaves, but the full continuous integral remains `total_power`. The Cell-aligned `source_rate` and `sink_rate` fields store this local rate density.

No per-world or per-Site discrete renormalization is performed. Cell-center Voronoi quadrature is allowed to have a small discretization error. This is also essential for boundary Sites: a Core is retained when its circle intersects the formal extent, but only the in-world part contributes. Missing power outside the crop is not forced back into the visible map.

## Transport is unchanged

The saturation terms remain:

```text
Source:  P(1-C)
Sink:   -KC
Forcing: P(1-C) - KC
```

The complete v2.3 equation, `mu = C-B` diffusion driver, Background Restoration, Flowability, Arcane Drift, upwind transport, open boundary, convergence, and stability synthesis are unchanged. Forcing remains a cell-local mass source/sink. `P+K` remains part of the explicit local removal rate used by CFL.

No Solver or Environment parameter was changed. In particular: Leyline radius 55, ambient Flowability 0.15, diffusion 1.0, drift speed 0.75, restoration 0.03/0.15, CFL safety 0.40, maximum `dt` 1.0, 400 iterations, epsilon `1e-5`, stability resistance 0.15, and stress response 2.0 are retained.
